Donate Blood

Save Lives

Mass Blood Donation is a community initiative that unites people through the simple yet meaningful act of donating blood. By bringing donors together, we help replenish vital blood supplies, support patients in need, and make life-saving transfusions possible. Every donation counts, and every donor makes a difference.

What is MBD?

Mass Blood Donation (MBD) is a community-based humanitarian initiative that encourages individuals to participate in organized blood donation drives to help maintain an adequate and accessible supply of blood for hospitals and medical institutions. It brings together eligible volunteer donors, healthcare professionals, and community organizations to collect blood safely and efficiently, addressing the continuous demand for blood needed in medical treatments, emergency situations, and life-saving procedures.

Why Blood Donation Matters

Blood donation helps maintain an adequate blood supply for hospitals and medical facilities. Donated blood is essential for treating accident victims, surgical patients, individuals with blood disorders, and mothers experiencing childbirth complications. Every donation can help save lives and support those in critical need.

The Blood Donation Process

Blood donation involves four simple steps: registration, health screening, blood collection, and recovery. Healthcare professionals guide donors throughout the process to ensure their safety, comfort, and proper care before and after donating.

The Impact of Your Donation

A single whole blood donation can be separated into different components, such as red blood cells, plasma, and platelets. These components can help multiple patients with different medical needs, making every successful donation a valuable contribution to saving lives.

Donor Benefits

Benefits of participating in mass blood donation programs.

Free Health Screening

Donors receive a mini-physical checking pulse, blood pressure, body temperature, and hemoglobin levels.

Blood Testing Insights

Donated blood is tested for infectious diseases, and donors are notified if anything unusual is found.

Cardiovascular Health

Regular donation can help reduce blood viscosity and lower iron stores, which studies link to a reduced risk of heart attacks and strokes.

Calorie Burn

The process of donating blood burns roughly 650 calories as the body works to replenish its blood supply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get the answers to the most common questions about blood donation and MBD programs.

Preparation and Eligibility

Who is eligible to donate?

Individuals aged 16 to 65 years old who weigh at least 110 lbs (50 kg) and are in good general health on the day of the donation are eligible. Donors who are 16 or 17 years old will require a signed parental consent form.

Sino-sino ang pwedeng mag-donate ng dugo?

Maaaring mag-donate ang mga indibidwal na may edad 16 hanggang 65 taong gulang, may timbang na hindi bababa sa 110 lbs o 50 kg, at pangkalahatang malusog ang pakiramdam sa araw ng donasyon.

How should I prepare for a mass blood donation event?

Get 6 to 8 hours of sleep the night before your donation. Drink plenty of water or juice to stay hydrated, and eat a healthy meal before arriving, while strictly avoiding fatty or greasy foods. Do not drink alcohol for 24 hours prior to donating.

Ano ang mga dapat gawin bago pumunta sa donation site?

Siguraduhing may 6 hanggang 8 oras na tulog sa gabi bago ang donasyon. Uminom ng maraming tubig, kumain ng sapat, at iwasan ang mamantika na pagkain at alak.

Can I donate if I have a tattoo, body piercing, or recently received a vaccine?

For tattoos and piercings, a 12-month deferral period from the date of the procedure is typically required to ensure safety. For vaccinations, eligibility depends entirely on the specific type of vaccine received; you should disclose this to the screening medical officer on-site.

Puwede bang mag-donate kung may tattoo, piercing, o bakuna?

Para sa tattoo at piercing, karaniwang may 12 buwang paghihintay mula sa araw ng pagkaka-tattoo. Para sa mga bakuna, depende ito sa uri kaya mas mabuting itanong sa screening officer.

Blood Donation Process

How long does the entire blood donation process take?

The actual blood extraction takes only 10 to 15 minutes. However, the entire process—including registration, medical history screening, a brief physical exam, and post-donation recovery—takes about 45 minutes to an hour.

Gaano katagal ang buong proseso ng pagkuha ng dugo?

Ang mismong pagkuha ng dugo ay tumatagal lamang ng 10 hanggang 15 minuto, ngunit ang buong proseso kasama ang screening ay maaaring umabot ng 45 minuto hanggang 1 oras.

Does donating blood hurt?

You will feel a brief pinch or sting (similar to a quick mosquito bite) when the needle is inserted. This minor discomfort subsides quickly, and the rest of the extraction process is generally painless.

Masakit ba ang mag-donate ng dugo?

Makakaramdam ka ng bahagyang kurot o tusok na parang kagat ng langgam kapag ipinasok ang karayom, ngunit mabilis ding mawawala ang sakit na ito.

How much blood is drawn during the session?

Medical staff will collect 450 milliliters (approximately one bag) of blood. This is a standard, safe amount that represents less than 10% of the average adult's total blood volume.

Gaano karaming dugo ang kinukuha sa bawat donor?

Karaniwang 450 mililitro o mga isang bag ng dugo ang kinukuha. Ito ay ligtas na halaga at maliit na bahagi lamang ng kabuuang dugo sa iyong katawan.

After the Donation

What should I do or avoid immediately after donating?

Rest in the recovery area for 10 to 15 minutes and enjoy the complimentary snacks and fluids provided. For the rest of the day, avoid heavy lifting or strenuous exercise, leave the bandage on for a few hours, and continue drinking plenty of liquids.

Ano ang dapat kong gawin o iwasan pagkatapos mag-donate?

Manatiling nakaupo o nakahiga muna ng 10 hanggang 15 minuto. Iwasang magbuhat ng mabibigat, huwag gumawa ng mabibigat na ehersisyo, at uminom ng maraming likido.

Will I feel dizzy or faint after giving blood?

Some donors experience mild lightheadedness, which is completely normal and quickly resolved by resting, drinking fluids, and eating a small snack. If you feel dizzy, sit down or lie down immediately with your feet elevated until it passes.

Makakaramdam ba ako ng pagkahilo pagkatapos?

Ang ilang donors ay nakakaramdam ng bahagyang pagkahilo. Normal ito at nawawala rin pagkatapos uminom ng likido at kumain ng meryenda.

How quickly does my body replenish the donated blood?

Your body replaces the lost plasma (fluid portion) within 24 to 48 hours. It takes about 4 to 6 weeks for your bone marrow to fully replace all the donated red blood cells.

Gaano kabilis mapapalitan ng katawan ang ibinigay na dugo?

Ang plasma o likidong bahagi ay napapalitan sa loob ng 24 hanggang 48 oras, habang ang mga red blood cells naman ay tumatagal ng 4 hanggang 6 na linggo.

Myths and Misconceptions

Can I contract an infection or virus like HIV or Hepatitis by donating blood?

Absolutely not. The blood donation process is exceptionally safe because sterile, single-use, disposable needles and equipment are used for every single donor and discarded immediately afterward.

Maaari bang makakuha ng sakit gaya ng HIV o Hepatitis sa pagdo-donate?

Hindi po. Ligtas ang pag-donate dahil ang lahat ng gagamiting karayom at kagamitan ay bago, sterile, at ginagamit lamang nang isang beses.

Can women donate blood during their menstrual period?

Yes, as long as you feel well. If you are not experiencing severe cramps or weakness, and your hemoglobin levels pass the on-site screening test, having your period does not disqualify you from donating.

Bawal ba mag-donate ang mga may regla?

Kung ikaw ay may regla ngunit malakas ang pakiramdam at sapat ang iyong hemoglobin level, maaari kang mag-donate.

Does donating blood cause a person to gain weight?

No, it does not. There is no medical link between giving blood and gaining weight. In fact, your body burns a small number of calories as it works to naturally synthesize and replenish new blood cells.

Nakakataba ba ang pag-donate ng dugo?

Hindi po ito nakakataba. Walang medikal na basehan ito; sa katunayan, nakakatulong pa ito sa pag-burn ng kaunting calories habang gumagawa ng bagong dugo ang katawan.

Impact and Health Benefits

How often can a person safely donate blood?

Healthy individuals can donate whole blood every three months (90 days). This window ensures your body has adequate time to completely rebuild its red blood cell count.

Gaano kadalas maaaring mag-donate ng dugo?

Maaaring mag-donate ng dugo tuwing ikatlong buwan o bawat 90 araw upang mabigyan ng sapat na panahon ang katawan na makabuo ng bagong dugo.

Are there any personal health benefits to donating blood?

Yes, donating blood helps reduce harmful iron stores in the body, which can lower the risk of cardiovascular disease. You also receive a free mini-physical exam that checks your blood pressure, pulse, and hemoglobin levels.

Ano ang mga personal na benepisyo sa kalusugan ng pagdo-donate?

Nakakatulong ito upang mabawasan ang labis na iron sa katawan na nagpapababa ng risk sa sakit sa puso. May kasama rin itong libreng basic health checkup.

How many lives can be saved with just one blood donation?

One single bag of blood can save up to three lives. This is because your whole blood can be separated into its distinct components—red blood cells, plasma, and platelets—to help multiple patients with different medical needs.

Ilang buhay ang maaari kong maisalba sa isang bag ng dugo?

Ang isang bag ng dugo ay maaaring makapagligtas ng hanggang tatlong buhay dahil maaari itong ihiwalay sa red cells, platelets, at plasma.
